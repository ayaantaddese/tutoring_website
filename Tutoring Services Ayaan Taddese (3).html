Frequently asked questions

Do you tutor in person?

No, sessions are online only, over video call with a shared digital whiteboard for working through problems together in real time.

What ages or grade levels do you work with?

Mainly high school students in math, SAT prep, and the IB Diploma Programme. Mentorship sessions are open to any age.

Can you help with IB Internal Assessments?

Yes, especially for Economics commentaries and the Business Management IA, where I can walk through structure, criteria, and drafts.

What do I need for a session?

Just a laptop with a camera and mic, and whatever materials you're working from: a textbook, a past paper, or your own notes.

How far in advance should I book?

Sooner is better, especially close to exam season, but reach out even if it's last-minute. I'll do what I can to fit you in.
